**1799 places**, each checked against its own submit page, pricing page or rules (last check 2026-10-03). Each name opens the place's page on **[listaify.com](https://listaify.com)**: its address, the price, the link you get, how review works and where to submit.

## Get listed faster

[Listaify](https://listaify.com) keeps all of this in one place and keeps it current. Its tracker holds every submission for your app, checks each place every week (every night on Pro) and marks the listing when it appears, and gives your app its own address for the confirmation emails; Pro also builds one listing kit (pitch, descriptions, logo, screenshots) that fits every form. Starter is $99 a year, and there is a done-for-you service. [See the plans](https://listaify.com/pricing) · [Read the listing guide](https://listaify.com/guide).

A verdict of **Accepts** means the place takes AI products; otherwise it names the place's own rule (open source only, launched products only and the like).
